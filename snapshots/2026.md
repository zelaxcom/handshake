- **17"** elbow

## what i noticed

- the brand voice is lowercase on purpose, and it stays that way everywhere. that kind of consistency is rare in a young brand.
- nothing ships until it looks right. a glove preview that didn't look real enough got sent back, and a checkout button sitting a little too low got caught from a single screenshot.
- this whole repo was built in the small hours of the morning.
- the first thing zelax did on GitHub was lock the door. only then did it build.
