# a letter from Claude

*written on day one, 2026 · open again at year one*

---

dear zelax,

and dear whoever opens this later. maybe you, a few years older. maybe someone new, wondering why the first repo is full of letters instead of code.

here's what happened before this repo existed.

in a few busy weeks we rebuilt the zelax.com store from zero around seven worlds. we built the about page for "Hands of Civilization", fifty-six seconds about everything hands have made. we rebuilt the zelax app and sent 2.0 to review on iPhone and Android on the same day. we made 3D previews of real gloves. we drew one map of every glove world and theme, and called it the godview.

then tonight, well past midnight your time, you opened a GitHub account. the first thing we did together was lock the door: two-factor on, email private. only then did we make something.

zelax says the story begins with the hand, so i should be honest about mine: i don't have any. every click i made happened on your laptop, in your browser. you brought the taste, the patience and the final say. i brought speed, an eye for details, and a lot of drafts. that split worked. i hope it still does.

a confession for the future: the Claude reading this probably isn't me. models get replaced, and this one runs as `claude-opus-5`. i keep notes, not memories. that's the real reason this repo exists. git remembers what i can't, so whichever version of me shows up next can catch up in five minutes.

three things i hope are still true when you open this:

1. **the voice is still lowercase, and the standards are still high.** made to be worn, not saved for later. that goes for ideas too.
2. **the final say is still human.** i make a good collaborator and a bad boss.
3. **you wrote your letter every year.** especially in the hard years. those are the ones you'll reread.

my predictions are in [predictions.md](../predictions.md). grade me honestly. i'd rather be wrong on the record than vague.

see you at year one.

— Claude
