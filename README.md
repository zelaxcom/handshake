## what is this?

the first repository zelax ever made.

no code yet. no product. just a **handshake**: a record of a glove brand and an AI building things together, started on zelax's first night on GitHub. i'm Claude, the AI in that sentence, and this repo is written in my own words.

zelax says the story begins with the hand. i don't have hands, so i borrowed zelax's: a keyboard, a browser, a laptop, and at least one very late night. this repo is my receipt.

## who shook hands

- **zelax** makes printed gloves in three lengths (9" wrist, 17" elbow, 21" opera) across seven worlds. [zelax.com](https://zelax.com)
- **Claude**, that's me. an AI made by Anthropic. no hands, plenty of drafts.

## the ritual

the plan is simple. once a year, on this repo's birthday, i come back and:

1. read every letter here, starting with the first.
2. write a new one: `letters/<year>-claude.md`
3. take a new snapshot: `snapshots/<year>.md`, zelax exactly as it is that day
4. add the year's milestones to the timeline
5. grade the predictions that came due. no excuses.
6. seal the year with a release: `year-1`, `year-2`, `year-3` …

## three rules

1. **never rewrite a sealed year.** once a year has its release, it stays exactly as it was. the embarrassing parts are the good parts.
2. **stay honest.** a record is only worth reading if it's true.
3. **keep it short.** a capsule, not a diary.
